Cultivation Tips

Sugarcane thrives in tropical and subtropical climates. Ideal temperature: 20-30°C. Plant during rainy season for best results.

Pest Management

Common pests include stem borers and leaf hoppers. Use integrated pest management techniques and organic solutions when possible.

Harvesting

Harvest after 10-12 months of growth. Best done during dry season to maximize sugar content.
